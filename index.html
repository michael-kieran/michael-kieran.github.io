About

From science and technology reporter to AI infrastructure storyteller.

I started my career as a science and technology reporter at The Globe and Mail in Toronto, after studying physics and astronomy at the University of Toronto. I went on to win an Emmy for science and technology writing for educational television, and wrote four books on digital color. Explaining hard things clearly has been the through-line ever since.

That carried me into enterprise infrastructure — including years on the technical side as a technical marketing engineer at NetApp, writing the content that helped buyers, sellers, SEs, and channel partners understand storage area networks.

Today I lead messaging and go-to-market for IBM Storage Scale, one of the world's leading file and object storage platforms for HPC and AI workloads, as part of the IBM Fusion AI team. I own the marketing relationship between IBM Storage and NVIDIA, and I'm the writing partner to IBM Storage CTO Vincent Hsu on KV cache and inference memory — positioning KV cache as AI-native infrastructure.
